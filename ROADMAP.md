# HomeBase — Feature Roadmap

Features discussed but saved for future phases. Don't lose these ideas!

---

## Phase 2 — Family Expansion

- **Points & Rewards System** — kids earn points for completing tasks, parents set point values per chore
- **Allowance / Cash-Out** — attach a dollar value to tasks, points tally up on kid's profile, cash out when threshold is hit
- **Shared Shopping List** enhancements — quantity, store name, sort by category
- **Bulletin Board** — post household announcements to everyone (not tasks, just messages)
- **Home Maintenance Log** — track when things were last done (oil change, HVAC filter, pest control), set next-due reminder
- **Family Goals** — shared goals with progress tracker (e.g. "Save for vacation", "Finish backyard by summer")
- **Task Templates** — pre-built chore lists parents can load in one tap (weekly cleaning, school routine, etc.)

---

## Phase 3 — Smart Features

- **Geofencing / Store Alerts** — detect when someone enters a store and send a push: "Don't forget the shopping list!"
- **Location Sharing** — admins can see where family members are (opt-in, privacy controls)
- **Morning Digest Notification** — scheduled daily push at set time: "Good morning! Here are your tasks for today"
- **Auto-assign Chores** — rotate chores automatically among household members on a schedule
- **Meal Planning** — weekly dinner planner, ties into shopping list

---

## Phase 4 — Polish & Publishing

- **Google Sign In** — one-tap login (requires standalone build, not needed for Expo Go)
- **App Store Publishing** — requires Apple Developer account ($99/year)
- **Onboarding flow** — tutorial for new families
- **Profile photos** — upload a photo for each family member
- **Dark mode** — full dark theme option
- **Multiple households** — for extended families or managing a second household
- **In-app notifications center** — history of all notifications received
- **Quiet hours** — no notifications sent between set hours (e.g. 10pm–7am)

---

## Notes

- Keep the UI simple, warm, and personality-driven
- Dad's "Simple Mode" should always be one tap from any screen
- Admin dashboard should always show a complete household overview at a glance
- Emergency button must always be accessible regardless of role
